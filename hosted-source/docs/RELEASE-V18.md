## Concrete workflow improvements

- Repair Command now has one subject focus shared between the home next-action card and the review board. Action counts are keyboard-accessible group filters. Future delayed checks remain scheduled; a quiet subject never borrows another subject's action or implies mastery.
- Every source has a private, read-only work brief for its actual verifier stage: diagnose, repair, disputed source, reattempt, fresh transfer, delayed check, scope inspection or recovery. It includes the exact source and linked-check references, honest marks and the earliest delayed date. Repair answers and diagnoses are deliberately excluded from the portable work brief so it can support an independent attempt. Briefs do not save evidence, complete retests, start timers or schedule reminders.
- The brief opens the exact source and relevant field in the existing verification desk, or the original record in the existing evidence ledger. Eligible check stages can prepare the existing attempt form with the source ID, subject, topic and check type. The user must enter the real completed result. Scores, closed-book and freshness confirmations are never inserted.
- The handoff refuses an already-open editor, pending/unsubmitted draft, unsupported draft, paused core save or missing source. It points to the existing draft recovery controls and leaves every field and original byte unchanged. A check logged this way still needs explicit selection in the matching verification chain; the existing verifier keeps its complete chronology, full-credit, fresh-question and scope gates.
- Historical attempts may legitimately lack a current syllabus topic. An exact handoff now also refuses missing, nonexistent, noncanonical or wrong-subject topic keys without opening/resetting the attempt form or writing records. The original remains accessible. The existing manual log remains available to choose the actual topic after reviewing the source; no unrelated first topic is invented. A valid canonical topic still passes through exactly.
- The paper-analysis and coverage tool is discoverable in the primary navigation and the home actions. Its evidence sourcing, analysis module and independent QA are documented by the root implementation. No private source corpus is bundled through these links.
