## Reproduced defects and corrections

1. A newly assigned attempt ID and an unseen checkbox could qualify the original question as fresh transfer and retention. Verification now requires distinct actual question references at transfer and delayed stages. Unicode/case/punctuation-equivalent references are caught; semantic difference still requires human judgment.
2. Raw full marks could override lower trusted marks, recorded errors and non-finite/coerced scores. Passing checks now reject those cases.
3. An explicitly linked later independent failed check left an older chain labelled retained. A later calendar-day failure now reopens the chosen weakness. Same-day chronology and unlinked failures are not inferred; the existing Reopen action remains necessary for those cases.
4. The attempt form accepted impossible dates such as 30 February 2026. Date validation now round-trips calendar values and validates the assessment date.
5. Seven manual checklist clicks announced a closed evidence loop without assessed work. The checklist now says self-recorded workflow progress and routes the final action to the Verification desk. It does not award retention. Automatically supplied placeholder question descriptions were removed; learners enter actual references.

The seven-day verification delay is unchanged and remains a product rule, not a scientifically universal optimum. Statuses are derived again from preserved records; an old unsupported retained label may cease qualifying without deleting any evidence. The broader core retest ledger and verification desk remain separate systems; this change does not pretend to unify every state machine.
