# V8 — Evidence & Capacity Edition

Adds a browser-private calendar snapshot projection to Command. The original Calendar remains the execution clock and canonical academic records remain external. Imported Asia/Colombo events are clipped to the selected date, overlapping reservations are flagged, and protected/fixed intervals are removed from study windows. Elapsed time is excluded on the current date and past dates receive no new work; remaining-time suggestions refresh each minute when controls are not active. Proposed question-level loops fit within existing study reservations and an explicit work budget with 20% reserve. Reservations and suggestions never create marks, completed attempts, mastery or Calendar events.

Candidate work respects due-check prerequisites, completed delayed checks and disputed-key holds. Higher-confidence misses receive repair priority. Suggestions use existing evidence; no sample scores or private workspace contents are shipped. Snapshots include their fetch date, window and staleness notice, and require refresh after Calendar changes. They do not synchronize automatically or cloud-save. Existing attempt and cloud namespaces remain untouched. Import validation, backups and concurrent-tab checks protect timetable copies. Share-safe mode hides timetable and suggestion contents. Offline precache includes the new assets and the preserved verification scripts.

Research and design references: official RemNote Exam Scheduler and Anki manual; retrieval/spacing review PMID 37615780; spaced-repetition meta-analysis PMID 41601436; sleep/memory review PMID 23589831. These support specific practices, not a clinical claim or a validated outcome for this whole app. No compulsory 15-hour study target or automatic recall-probability estimate is introduced.

Validation: JavaScript syntax, TypeScript, protected-time/cross-midnight/budget/retention/hold tests, full regression suite and production artifact checks. Browser visual and interactive QA unavailable; no browser verification claimed.
