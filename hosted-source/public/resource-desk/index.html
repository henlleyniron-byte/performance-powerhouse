PRACTICAL ANSWER CHECK

Show the method, not just the result.

  • Label apparatus and identify the measured quantities.
  • State controlled variables and realistic assumptions.
  • Show the relationship used; label graph axes and units.
  • Explain gradient or intercept and repeat useful measurements.
  • Name the specific error and a practical improvement.

Practise these steps from memory. “Avoid human error” does not name a useful improvement.
