Optional diagnostic sessions

Solo mode is the default. A small group is optional, and every learner keeps their own records. No leaderboard, invitations, cloud upload or peer data collection. A session plan does not create an attempt.

  1. 0–5: define target
  2. 5–35: closed-book timed attempt
  3. 35–50: strict marking
  4. 50–60: diagnose
  5. 60–75: minimum repair
  6. 75–85: reattempt and fresh transfer
  7. 85–90: schedule retests

Use this instead of passive study; adjust to available time and recovery needs. Optional roles: timekeeper, marker and logger. No Calendar events are created.
