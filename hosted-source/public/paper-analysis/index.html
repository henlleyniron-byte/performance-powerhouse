Turn a pattern into a marked output
  1. Choose an actual paper and exact MCQ references. Check the syllabus and variant.
  2. Attempt the set closed-book under the paper's authentic time allowance; record confidence and guesses separately.
  3. Use the correct official marking scheme. Diagnose the reasoning or knowledge error; repair from the prescribed resource.
  4. Reattempt closed-book, test a fresh transfer question, then schedule and complete a delayed retest.
