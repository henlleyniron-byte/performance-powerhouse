Your first real attempt · about 10 minutes to learn the workflow

Make one attempt count.

Use this guide while learning the controls, then complete an actual closed-book timed question. Reading this guide or checking a box never becomes an exam result.

For Sri Lankan English-medium A/L Biology, Chemistry and Physics. Start with one question or a short set; a full paper is optional. Protect sleep and recovery.

01 · SOURCE

Choose the actual question.

Record the subject, source, year or worksheet, and exact question identifier. Open your authorised paper or teacher-provided material. Do not claim an official source without checking it.

Example (synthetic only):
Physics · practice worksheet A · question 7. No score is claimed.
02 · ATTEMPT

Work independently.

Choose a time limit and keep notes, AI and solution keys closed. A timer is only a condition; the evidence is the attempted answer.

Important: if assistance was used, label it as assisted practice, not an independent attempt.

03 · MARK

Use the real scheme.

After stopping the clock, mark strictly against an official, teacher, or clearly attributed marking source. Record the raw marks and total possible marks.

A guessed correct answer may earn the raw mark if the scheme allows it, but its underlying understanding remains fragile. Do not silently deduct it from the official raw score.

04 · DIAGNOSE

Find the exact weak link.

Distinguish knowledge gap, reasoning error, misread instruction, calculation slip, timing issue and guess-based understanding. Note the smallest fixable cause.

If the answer key is disputed or unclear, mark it verification hold and do not invent certainty.

05 · REPAIR

Repair narrowly, then close the source.

Relearn only the specific concept or step that failed. Do a fresh closed-book reattempt. Correction while looking at the answer is not verification.

Keep the academic error in your existing Notion Error Bank; meaningful behavioural or workflow mistakes belong in your existing Mistake Vault. Do not create a competing canonical database.

06 · TRANSFER + RETEST

Prove it survives.

Try an unseen related question. Later, do an independent delayed check. Keep the status open until the relevant stages pass; reopen it if a check fails.

Schedule future time in Calendar. Record actual outcomes in Notion and/or an explicitly exported Powerhouse record. A scheduled item is not proof of completion.

Pre-flight checklist (temporary on this page)

These boxes are just a reminder and are not saved. Their state never changes your Powerhouse marks, evidence or Notion records.

What to put in “Log evidence”

  1. Actual date, subject, source and question — not a planned session or fabricated worksheet mark.
  2. Conditions — time used, closed-book versus assisted.
  3. Raw score/denominator — according to the cited scheme; keep guessed-but-correct separate as an uncertainty note.
  4. Diagnosed error — one specific cause and a traceable source.
  5. Next step — closed-book reattempt, unseen transfer or delayed retest, whichever is truly pending.
